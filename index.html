Live Scam Alerts

Common threats reported across West Africa

These examples show the types of scams CYBerShield can help citizens recognize, report, and avoid.

High Risk

Mobile Money Reversal Scam

Scammers claim they mistakenly sent money to your account and ask for your mobile money PIN or OTP to reverse the transaction.

  • Never share your PIN or OTP.
  • Call your provider directly.
  • Do not approve unknown prompts.
Medium Risk

Fake Job Processing Fee

Fraudsters send fake job offers and ask applicants to pay a processing, training, or recruitment fee before an interview.

  • Verify the company website.
  • Do not pay before interview.
  • Check the recruiter’s email address.
High Risk

WhatsApp Code Takeover

Attackers ask for your WhatsApp verification code, then use it to take over your account and scam your contacts.

  • Never share verification codes.
  • Enable two-step verification.
  • Warn contacts if compromised.

What should you report?

Suspicious links

Links asking you to verify accounts, claim prizes, or update details.

Mobile money fraud

Messages asking for your PIN, OTP, approval, or reversal assistance.

Fake opportunities

Jobs, scholarships, investments, or travel offers asking for payment.

Account takeover attempts

Anyone asking for your WhatsApp, email, or social media verification code.
